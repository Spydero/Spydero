## About me

I'm **Jyothiranand S.**, a full-stack software engineer in Pune. I build TypeScript backends with **Express** and **Hono**, NestJS when the product needs it, and the **Next.js** apps that sit on top of them, backed by **PostgreSQL** and **Prisma**.

I spent **six months at Axium Studios LLP** (Mar to Sep 2026) shipping SaaS across freight logistics and healthcare, including LLM-powered features and agent-style workflows. Outside that, I keep two public products you can clone and run: **[Ternline](https://github.com/Spydero/Freight-SaaS)** (shipping console) and **[Nora](https://github.com/Spydero/nora)** (evidence-cited health guidance).

Currently pursuing a **B.Com**. Open to full-time backend or full-stack roles.
